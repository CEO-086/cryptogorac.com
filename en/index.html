About us

CryptoGorac.com was founded in 2025. We grew out of years of collaboration on various projects, which led us to the idea of forming our own professional team. As a software studio, Cryptogorac is built on Microsoft technologies, but it also uses other technologies and acquires new knowledge every day, so it can give its clients and modern businesses the best and most secure service possible.

Our team is certified by the most eminent universities and companies in the blockchain and crypto industry. The purpose of the crypto education section is to grow, over time, into an informative and educational portal focused on the crypto market, as well as on the IT industry as a whole.

How we work

We are built on Microsoft technology, but we are ready to tackle your requirements across a range of challenging technologies. Our services span IT consulting, marketing and planning all the way to a finished digital product. Our product and project managers, as well as our software architects, are experienced in end-to-end project management, so we also offer maintenance and upgrade services after a project launches.
